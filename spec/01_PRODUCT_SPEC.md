Define a simple mobile app that converts physical business cards into searchable digital contacts. 
The primary user is a professional who frequently networks and receives visiting cards. V1 must solve one problem reliably: capture a card, extract its details using free on-device OCR, let the user verify them, and save the contact locally. 
The app must work without account creation, internet dependency, or paid APIs. Keep scope intentionally small. 
V1 includes scanning, OCR extraction, editing, local storage, search, contact viewing, and optional phone-contact export. 
Success means fast scanning, accurate extraction, easy correction, and reliable later retrieval.
