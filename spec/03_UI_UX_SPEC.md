Use a minimal, clean, professional light-mode interface designed for fast scanning.
Keep navigation simple with Home, Contacts, and Settings only where necessary.
Home should prominently provide a Scan Card action and show recently saved contacts. The scan flow should require minimal taps: capture, process, review, save. 
Use clear typography, generous spacing, simple icons, restrained visual styling, and accessible contrast. Avoid decorative elements, gradients, excessive animations, or crowded screens. 
Every important action needs visible feedback. Design responsively for common Android phones first. 
Dark mode is explicitly out of scope until the light-mode V1 is tested and approved.
