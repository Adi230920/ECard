V1 contains five core capabilities: scan a business card, extract text with OCR, convert extracted text into contact fields, verify/edit details, and save/search contacts locally. 
Contact fields include name, designation, company, phone, email, website, address, LinkedIn, notes, scan date, and original card image. 
Users can open saved contacts and use available phone, email, website, or WhatsApp actions. 
Users may export a contact to the device address book. 
Include empty states, OCR failure handling, duplicate warnings, and delete confirmation. 
Do not add login, cloud sync, AI, CRM features, subscriptions, analytics, or dark mode in V1.
